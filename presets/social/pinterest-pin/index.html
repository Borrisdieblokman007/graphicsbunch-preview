    Pinterest’s business spec for a standard image recommends a 2:3 ratio, or 1000 × 1500 pixels. Pins taller than 2:3 can be cut off in the feed. The same 1000 × 1500 size is repeated for full-bleed showcase creatives.

    That page is an ad spec, and it is the pixel size Pinterest itself publishes. Organic pins use the same feed crop. JPEG or PNG, up to 20 MB on desktop in that table.

    A square product photo becomes a short pin and loses the vertical space Pinterest gives you. This preset cover-crops to 2:3. Use contain if the whole product must stay visible, and accept side padding.

    Tips for this size

    • Put the product in the upper two thirds. The bottom of a pin is where descriptions overlap on some surfaces.
    • Do not upload a 9:16 story file and hope the extra length shows. Pinterest will clip it.
    • 1000 pixels on the short side is enough for the feed. You do not need a 4K pin.
