    Meta’s Instagram content publishing reference does not say “always use 1080”. It says a feed image must be JPEG, at most 8 MB, sRGB, with an aspect ratio from 4:5 to 1.91:1. Width must be at least 320 and at most 1440. Wider files are scaled down.

    1440 × 1440 is 1:1, inside that ratio, at the maximum width Meta says it will keep. A 1080 square also fits, but it is not the ceiling. This preset uses the ceiling so the file is not needlessly reduced.

    If you share a reel to the feed, Meta says it crops the cover to the middle square. A square master avoids that surprise.

    Tips for this size

    • Export JPEG. The publishing reference does not list PNG for feed images.
    • Stay at or under 8 MB. A 1440 square JPEG at quality 86 is usually well under that.
    • Do not add a second page for “1080 square”. It is the same 1:1 job at a smaller width.
