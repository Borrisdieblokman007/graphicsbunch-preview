    Already have a licence key?

    Google’s image_link specification recommends images around 1500 × 1500 pixels or larger. The hard ceiling is 64 megapixels and 16 MB. Accepted types are JPEG, WebP, PNG, GIF, BMP and TIFF, and the file extension has to match the bytes.

    A new floor of 500 × 500 for every product starts on 31 January 2027. Until then, non-clothing can be as small as 100 × 100 and clothing 250 × 250, but those sizes already warn. YouTube Shopping on TV wants 500 × 500 now. This preset uses the recommended 1500 square so you are not rebuilding the feed in 2027.

    Google asks for a clear view of the product, with the item taking roughly 75 to 90 percent of the frame. Do not submit a thumbnail you have blown up.

    Tips for this size

    • Match the extension to the format. A PNG named .jpg can be rejected as an unsupported type.
    • Leave a small margin. Google’s guidance is about 75 to 90 percent of the frame, not a crop through the product.
    • Apparel still has to read clearly at the old 250-pixel check, so do not rely on tiny text printed on the item.
