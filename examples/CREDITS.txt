Example pictures on the Graphics Bunch tools site
Created 24 Oct 2026 for this project.

Every picture is original artwork drawn for Graphics Bunch.
No store designs are used. No Unsplash, Pexels or other third-party photos are used.
Where a tool has a pixel function, that function from packages/engine was run on the drawing.
Files are PNG, WebP and AVIF. AVIF and WebP are the preferred sources. PNG is the fallback.

cutout: Original artwork of a mug on a table, and the same mug drawn on a checker so the cut-out reads. Not a Graphics Bunch store design. The live tool uses MODNet on your photo.
edge: Original star with a white semi-transparent fringe. The after file is that bitmap after engine defringe against white and hardenAlpha.
dpi: Original monogram. Before is a nearest-neighbour enlarge of a small copy, which is what a plain scale looks like. After is the crisp drawing, and the PNG is tagged 300 DPI with engine setPngDpi. The tag does not add detail.
compress: Same original mug scene. Before is a coarse copy standing in for a heavy, soft file. After is the clean drawing the converter would keep.
watermark: Original mug. The after picture tiles the word STUDIO, the same kind of text stamp the watermark tool draws.
mirror: Original wrap artwork. After is engine flipHorizontal, the mirror used for sublimation.
seam: Original tiles. The seamless tile scores 4.80 from engine seamScore (lower means the opposite edges agree). The before tile has a painted border so the join shows.
colour: Original white artwork. After is a multiply onto coral, the same blend the colour preview uses so white takes the blank colour.
template: Original wrap and a mug guide drawn for this page. It is not a press template and not a store design. The tool itself builds the PDF at the blank size.
sheet: Original sticker art, once, then repeated on an A4-shaped sheet the way the print sheet lays a gang.
svg: Original blocky art beside a clean star. The PNG to SVG tool traces flat shapes. This pair shows that idea. It is not output from a GPL tracer.
square: Original mug scene filling the frame, then the same scene inset with an even white border.
daylight: Original scene, darkened with a blue cast. After is engine enhanceRgba (grey-world balance, levels and a light sharpen).
shadow: Original mug on white. The ellipse uses engine contactShadow for the centre and radii. Amazon main images should stay without this shadow.
scene: Original mug on white, then the same drawing placed on a navy shelf drawn for this project. Not a stock photo and not a store design.
meta: Original diagram of a metadata report. The tool strips EXIF and GPS and keeps a JPEG profile. No real GPS coordinates are in the file.
name: Original before and after filenames. The helper builds a hyphenated product-colour-material name.
fee: Original estimate card. The calculator uses the rates cited on its page. This picture is not an invoice.
download: Original pass and fail cards for the digital download limits: 5 files, 20 MB, 70 safe characters.
pdf: Original page-size diagram. The checker reads the PDF on this device and does not edit it.
size: Original diagram. 8 inches at 300 DPI is 2400 pixels, from the print-size maths in the engine.
picker: Original flowers. The swatch is coral #F15957 from the Graphics Bunch palette, the sort of hex the picker copies.
pdfpack: Original pictures gathered onto one A4-shaped page.
recipe: Original sketch of loose files becoming one zip. Recipes chain the shared presets on this device.
heic: Original diagram of a phone HEIC becoming a JPEG. The converter decodes HEIC in the browser.
print: Original pass and warn marks for the print-ready checker.
profile: Original drawing of a square photo beside a circle crop on a colour ground with a ring. Not a platform logo and not a store design.
banner: Original drawing of a full photo beside a wide cover. The pale band marks a safe zone. It is not a logo and not a store design.
post: Original drawing of a photo fitted inside a taller frame so the edges stay visible. The spare space is a flat colour. Not a store design.
