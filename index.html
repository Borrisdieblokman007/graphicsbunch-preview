      04Questions

      Questions

      Do the photos leave this device?

      No. Files never leave this device. The tools read the pictures you choose and write new files for you to download. There is no account and no upload step.

      What can I do for free?

      Resize, compress, HEIC, watermark, square padding, auto enhance, EXIF removal and filename rename are free for 10 files at a time, 10 MB each. The marketplace check is free too. Background removal is one image, with the long edge at 1,024 pixels. A plain 2× enlarge is one image. AVIF export on this website is free.

      Where is the image resizer?

      Open Resize & Convert, then Image resizer. Presets cover Etsy, Pinterest, Instagram, paper and a T-shirt or tote. Drop a photo on this page and the three closest tools are suggested.

      What does Pro add on the website?

      A larger batch, a target file size, saved presets, background removal up to a 4,096 pixel long edge, the 4× model, the marketplace photo fix, a clean mockup, a contact shadow and on-device alt text. Recipes add custom chains, import and export, and a larger batch at full resolution. The WordPress plugin is a separate download: unlimited WebP on your server, with AVIF and auto enhance in an add-on.
