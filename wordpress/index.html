WebP on your server, originals kept

Graphics Bunch Image Optimizer is a free WordPress plugin. It creates WebP copies of media library uploads with GD or Imagick, and serves the WebP with a fallback. On JPEG and PNG uploads it also removes EXIF and GPS segments. The picture data is not recompressed by that step. There is no monthly quota. Compression and a maximum width and height are included. Bulk optimisation runs on your site, one image at a time.

AVIF, per-size rules, WP-CLI bulk commands, multisite network settings and Daylight auto enhance are not in the free plugin. They live in Graphics Bunch Image Optimizer Pro, a separate add-on. Auto enhance writes a separate JPEG only when the add-on licence filter is on. The free plugin keeps working if the add-on is absent. The add-on does not phone home in this build. Freemius is not connected, so the paid features are not applied.
