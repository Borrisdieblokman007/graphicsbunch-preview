Billing

Can I pay on this page today?

No. Polar is not connected, and no card details are collected. The checkout buttons stay disabled until that connection exists. There is no email field and no countdown.

Who charges VAT or sales tax?

When checkout is connected, Polar is the seller of record and adds VAT or sales tax. Graphics Bunch adds no fee on top. UK and EU prices include VAT. US sales tax, where it applies, is added at checkout.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

You cancel in the Polar billing portal. Refunds go through Polar. Licence activations are not enforced in this version. A key typed into a tool is not sent with analytics.

Does Pro upload my photos?

No. Pro raises limits inside the browser. The picture is still processed on this device.

Why is the annual plan highlighted?

It is the plan we suggest, at the same price for everyone. A presentation test is queued and is not running.

What does the WordPress add-on cost?

The plugin on your server is free for unlimited WebP. The separate add-on, not on sale yet, is planned at $39 a year for one site, $79 a year for five sites, and $99 lifetime.Read the WordPress page.
