    Questions

    Are the scenes stock photos?

    No. Linen, the navy shelf and the grey sweep are drawn in the browser for this project. There is no licensed photograph and no template pack to clear.

    Why is the free file watermarked?

    The mockup is a Pro export. The free run still places the product so you can judge the scene, and it tiles “Graphics Bunch preview” across the picture. Preview Pro on this device for a clean JPEG.

    Will this wrap a shirt or a mug?

    Mug and skinny tumbler are preview scenes: the photo is bent around a cylinder so you can judge the artwork. They are not press templates, and a shirt is not warped. For a print-at-100% wrap, use the template maker.
