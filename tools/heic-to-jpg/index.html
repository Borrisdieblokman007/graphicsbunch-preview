    Questions

    Why do iPhone photos fail on other sites?

    Many phones save HEIC. Marketplaces often want JPEG. This page decodes HEIC in the browser with libheif and writes a JPEG.

    Does this keep the original HEIC?

    Yes. The download is a new file. The HEIC you added is not deleted and is not uploaded.

    Can I convert a whole camera roll?

    Free use is 10 files at a time, 10 MB each. Run the tool again for the next batch, or preview Pro for a larger batch.
