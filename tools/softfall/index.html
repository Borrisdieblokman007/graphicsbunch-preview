    Questions

    Where should I use the shadow?

    On secondary listing images, a shop gallery or a social crop. Amazon main images are expected on pure white without a shadow, props or text. Keep the main image clean and use Softfall beside it.

    Does this reuse the background remover?

    It knocks out near-white pixels the same way a plain cut-out does, then paints the shadow under the remaining subject. It does not load the MODNet model. A busy background should be removed first.

    Why is the shadow Pro?

    The contact shadow is a paid control. Preview Pro on this device to draw it locally. The picture is still not uploaded.
