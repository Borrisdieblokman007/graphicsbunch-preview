    Questions

    Why is AVIF free here?

    The encoding runs on your device, so there is no server quota to meter. Some optimisers charge for AVIF. Graphics Bunch does not.

    What does the quality number mean?

    Higher keeps more detail and usually makes a larger file. 82 is a sound default for WebP and JPEG product photos. AVIF often looks clean at 50.

    Can I hit a target file size?

    Target size, such as under 200 KB, is a Pro control. The quality slider on this page is free.
