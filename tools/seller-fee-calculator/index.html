  • Etsy Help: payment processing fees. US 3% + $0.25. UK 4% + £0.20. Confirmed 18 Oct 2026. The same page states the 6.5% transaction fee.

Questions

Is this my invoice?

No. It is an estimate from the rates cited on the page, not tax or financial advice, and not a bill from the marketplace.

Which rates were confirmed from Etsy help?

Payment processing and the 6.5% transaction fee were read on 18 Oct 2026. Offsite Ads 15% and 12% with a $100 cap were read the same day. The UK regulatory fee page lists 0.48% and does not print 22 Jun 2026. The VAT page does not print 20%. Listing fees of $0.20 and £0.15 were not re-confirmed from etsy.com on this pass.
