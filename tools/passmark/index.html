    Rules last checked 2026-10-17

    The checker reads the photo on this device and compares it with the published listing rules below. A busy background is easier to judge after a cut-out. Painted words are not read.

    • Etsy. At least 635 px on the first photo, 2000 px recommended, sRGB, and files over 1 MB may not finish uploading. Transparent PNG areas appear black. Thumbnails are cropped to square, portrait and landscape, so leave a border. Etsy Help: photo tips for sellers
    • eBay. At least 500 px on the long side, about 1600 px recommended. No added borders, text or artwork. eBay UK and AU prohibit watermarks. This check does not read words painted into the photo. eBay picture policy
    • Amazon. Main image on a pure white background (255, 255, 255), product filling at least 85% of the frame, and at least 1000 px on the long side for zoom. Text and logos are not allowed on the main image. This check judges the white field and the fill, not painted words. Amazon Home Selection style guide
    • Shopify. A consistent 1:1 ratio, with 2048×2048 recommended. Use a descriptive hyphenated filename, and write alt text. Avoid names that end in thumb, small or large. Shopify product media typesShopify: image alt text

    How to use it

    1. 1Add up to 10 listing photos. The sample mug is a white-background product.

    3. 3Preview Pro, then use Fix to the rules for a white sRGB JPEG sized for that marketplace.

    Questions

    What does the free check measure?

    Pixel size, aspect ratio, file weight, JPEG or PNG, an sRGB colour profile when the file records one, and whether the border is white. It does not read words painted into the picture, so a watermark made of text can still pass the pixel check.

    What does the Pro fix change?

    It flattens the subject onto white, scales it to the fill that marketplace asks for, and writes an sRGB JPEG under that file-size guide. Etsy is a landscape frame, because Etsy recommends against square-only photos. Amazon, eBay and Shopify fixes are square.

    Where do the rules come from?

    Each marketplace block on this page links to the help page or style guide used. The date beside the list is the day those pages were last read for this tool.
