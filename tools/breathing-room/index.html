    Questions

    Why leave a border on a square?

    Etsy crops listing thumbnails to square, portrait and landscape. A product that touches the edge gets clipped in at least one of those frames. Shopify also recommends a consistent 1:1 product image. Padding keeps the object inside all three crops.

    How is the product found?

    Pixels that are not near-white are treated as the product. A busy background should be cut out first. This page does not call the background model.

    Is the square crop free?

    Yes. Free use is 10 files at a time, 10 MB each. Pro only raises that batch cap. There is no separate charge for the padding.
